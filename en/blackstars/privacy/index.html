Black Stars Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 July 2026

Black Stars is an offline tactical strategy game. It does not require an account, does not show advertising, and does not sell personal data.

1. Controller and contact

Black Stars is provided by ArnoHealth S.L. / onmedic. For privacy questions or to exercise your data-protection rights, contact privacy@onmedic.com.

2. Data processed by the game

Gameplay progress, settings, stars and best times are stored locally on your device. We do not operate player accounts or a game backend, so this local information is not sent to us.

To maintain reliability, the game uses Sentry for crash reporting and performance monitoring. When an error occurs, or as part of limited performance monitoring, Sentry may receive:

  • crash logs, diagnostic information and stack traces;
  • app performance data, such as app start and responsiveness information;
  • technical device and app information, including an app installation identifier, device model, operating-system version and app version; and
  • limited in-app interaction breadcrumbs needed to diagnose an error.

We do not intentionally send names, email addresses, contact lists, precise location, photos, messages, health data, financial-account data or advertising identifiers to Sentry.

3. Purchases

Black Stars offers one optional, non-recurring Google Play purchase to unlock the full campaign. Payments are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment-card details. The game only uses the purchase result to unlock the content on the device.

4. Why we process this data

Technical data is used solely to identify, diagnose and fix crashes, stability issues and performance problems. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in providing and maintaining a secure, functioning game.

5. Service providers and international transfers

Sentry processes diagnostic and performance data as our service provider. Sentry may process data in the European Economic Area and, where applicable, under appropriate safeguards for international transfers. We do not use this data for advertising or marketing and do not sell it.

6. Retention and security

Local game data remains on your device until you remove it by uninstalling the game or clearing its app data. Sentry retains diagnostic events according to the service configuration needed to investigate and resolve issues. Data sent to Sentry is encrypted in transit.

7. Children

Black Stars is rated for a general audience. It does not create user profiles or knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes

We may update this policy when the game or its data practices change. The current version will always be available at this address.
